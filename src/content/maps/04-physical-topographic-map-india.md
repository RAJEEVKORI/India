### Cartographic Specifications & Verification
* Details India's sovereign physical relief features including the Northern Himalayan Arc, Indo-Gangetic Plains, Thar Desert, Deccan Plateau, and Eastern & Western Ghats per the [Survey of India](https://onlinemaps.surveyofindia.gov.in/).
* Depicts India's complete 2,305,143 sq. km Exclusive Economic Zone (EEZ) maritime limits delineated by the [National Hydrographic Office](https://hydrobharat.gov.in/).
* Covers the Andaman & Nicobar Islands and Lakshadweep archipelagos with verified maritime baseline markers established by the [Ministry of Earth Sciences](https://www.moes.gov.in/).
* Complies with the Territorial Waters, Continental Shelf, Exclusive Economic Zone and other Maritime Zones Act per the [India Code Legislative Portal](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/).
