### Cartographic Specifications & Verification
* Delineates the authentic sovereign administrative boundaries of the Union Territory of Jammu and Kashmir and the Union Territory of Ladakh under the [Jammu and Kashmir Reorganisation Act, 2019](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/).
* Confirms the inclusion of the districts of Mirpur, Muzaffarabad, Gilgit, Gilgit Wazarat, Chilhas, Tribal Territory, and Leh & Kargil districts as published by the [Survey of India](https://onlinemaps.surveyofindia.gov.in/).
* Corrects foreign atlas distortions by reflecting true de jure statutory boundaries of the Republic of India registered with the [Survey of India](https://onlinemaps.surveyofindia.gov.in/).
* Includes high-resolution district-level demarcation and topography compliant with statutory gazette notifications of the [Ministry of Home Affairs](https://www.mha.gov.in/).
