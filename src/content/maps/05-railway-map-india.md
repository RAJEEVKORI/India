### Cartographic Specifications & Verification
* Official 23rd Edition (2026) prepared by the Survey of India based on operational network data supplied by the [Ministry of Railways (Railway Board)](https://indianrailways.gov.in/).
* Documents broad-gauge electrification status across all 18 Zonal Railways and division headquarters per the [Ministry of Railways](https://indianrailways.gov.in/).
* Delineates electrified double lines, single lines, gauge conversions, and key strategic railway corridors under the [Survey of India National Cartographic Registry](https://onlinemaps.surveyofindia.gov.in/).
* Available as official full-resolution vector PDF and 4K digital raster downloads directly compliant with the [Ministry of Railways](https://indianrailways.gov.in/).
