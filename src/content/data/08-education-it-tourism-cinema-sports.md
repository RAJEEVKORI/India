## 1. Human Capital, IT Services & Soft Power Facts
* India produces ~2.55 million STEM graduates annually (Rank #2 globally) and holds the highest female share of STEM graduates (~42.6%–43.0%) among all major economies per the [Ministry of Education All India Survey on Higher Education (AISHE)](https://aishe.gov.in/).
* India hosts over 1,700 Global Capability Centres (GCCs)—accounting for more than 53% of the world's total enterprise GCCs—generating USD 75.53 billion in 2026 alongside USD 205B–224B in direct IT software exports per [MeitY](https://www.meity.gov.in/) and [NASSCOM](https://nasscom.in/).
* India records over 2.51 billion annual domestic tourist visits (Rank #2 globally), ranks #39 on the WEF Travel & Tourism Development Index (#1 in South Asia), and holds 43 UNESCO World Heritage Sites (Rank #6 globally) per the [Ministry of Tourism](https://tourism.gov.in/) and [UNESCO](https://whc.unesco.org/).
* India is the world's #1 producer of theatrical feature films, certifying 1,700 to 2,400+ films annually across 20+ languages via the [Central Board of Film Certification (CBFC)](https://www.cbfcindia.gov.in/).
* India won historic double Gold medals (Open and Women) at the 2024 FIDE Chess Olympiad, produced the youngest World Chess Champion in history (Gukesh D, aged 18), holds 8 Olympic Field Hockey Gold medals (#1 all-time), and won the 2024 ICC T20 World Cup and 2025 Champions Trophy per the [Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports](https://yas.nic.in/).
