## 1. Ecology, Wildlife, Water & Space Exploration Facts
* India is home to 3,682 wild tigers (~75% of the world's wild tiger population across 55+ reserves), 674+ Asiatic lions (100% of the world's wild population), ~29,964 Asian elephants (~60% of the global population), and ~3,262+ Greater One-Horned Rhinoceroses (>81% of the global population) per the [National Tiger Conservation Authority (NTCA)](https://ntca.gov.in/) and [MoEFCC](https://moef.gov.in/).
* India ranks #3 globally in net annual forest area gain (+266,000 hectares per year), with total domestic forest and tree cover spanning 827,357 sq. km (25.17% of India's geographical area) per the [Forest Survey of India (FSI)](https://fsi.nic.in/) and [FAO Global Forest Resources Assessment](https://www.fao.org/forest-resources-assessment/en/).
* India ranks #1 in Asia and #3 globally with 101 designated Ramsar Wetlands of International Importance (spanning 14,005.12 sq. km as of August 2026) and ranks #1 globally in net irrigated agricultural area (~76.5 million hectares) per the [Ministry of Jal Shakti](https://jalshakti-dowr.gov.in/) and [Ramsar Convention Portal](https://www.ramsar.org/).
* Under the Jal Jeevan Mission, India has provided functional household tap water connections to over 15.2+ crore (152+ million) rural homes (>78.8% rural coverage, up from 16.7% in 2019) per the [Jal Jeevan Mission Dashboard](https://ejalshakti.gov.in/jjmreport/JJMIndia.aspx).
* India is the first nation to land a spacecraft near the Lunar South Pole (Chandrayaan-3 at ~USD 75M), the first Asian nation to reach Martian orbit on its maiden attempt (Mangalyaan at ~USD 74M), the fourth nation to achieve autonomous in-space docking (SPADEX), and has commercially launched 433+ foreign satellites for 34 countries per the [Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO)](https://www.isro.gov.in/).
