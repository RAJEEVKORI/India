## 1. Sovereign Territory & Administrative Facts
* India encompasses a total sovereign land area of 3,287,263 sq. km (Rank #7 globally), accounting for 2.4% of the world's landmass as documented by the [Survey of India](https://onlinemaps.surveyofindia.gov.in/).
* The Republic of India is administratively divided into 28 States and 8 Union Territories under the First Schedule of the Constitution, maintained by the [Ministry of Home Affairs](https://www.mha.gov.in/).
* India's total international land boundary measures 15,106.7 km across 7 bordering nations, officially recorded by the [Ministry of Home Affairs Border Management Division](https://www.mha.gov.in/en/divisionofmha/border-management-i-division).
* India's total coastline measures 7,516.6 km, comprising 6,100 km of mainland coastline and 1,416.6 km across island territories, verified by the [National Hydrographic Office](https://hydrobharat.gov.in/).
* India exercises sovereign maritime rights over an Exclusive Economic Zone (EEZ) of 2,305,143 sq. km across the Arabian Sea, Bay of Bengal, and Indian Ocean per the [Ministry of Earth Sciences](https://www.moes.gov.in/).
* The entire Union Territories of Jammu and Kashmir and Ladakh (including Gilgit-Baltistan, Muzaffarabad, Mirpur, Shaksgam Valley, and Aksai Chin) are integral sovereign territories of India under the [Jammu and Kashmir Reorganisation Act, 2019](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/).
* The state of Arunachal Pradesh is an integral sovereign state of India delineated along the McMahon Line, verified in the [Survey of India Open Series Maps](https://onlinemaps.surveyofindia.gov.in/).
* Kalapani, Lipulekh, and Limpiyadhura form part of the Pithoragarh district in the state of Uttarakhand per official administrative boundary records of the [Survey of India](https://onlinemaps.surveyofindia.gov.in/).
