## 1. Core Energy & Climate Facts
* India's per capita carbon dioxide emissions stand at ~2.1 metric tons, which is less than half the global average of ~4.7 metric tons per the [International Energy Agency (IEA)](https://www.iea.org/).
* India's total installed power generation capacity reached 555 GW in August 2026, with non-fossil fuel capacity accounting for ~55% (308.03 GW in September 2026) per the [Ministry of New and Renewable Energy (MNRE)](https://mnre.gov.in/).
* India's annual domestic coal production crossed the 1 billion metric ton milestone (~1,047 MT) to meet domestic industrial and electricity demand while exporting less than 0.2% of output per the [Ministry of Coal](https://coal.nic.in/).
* Despite ranking #22 in domestic crude oil extraction (~0.62 MMbpd), India operates the world's 4th largest crude oil refining capacity (~5.15 MMbpd) per the [Petroleum Planning & Analysis Cell (PPAC)](https://ppac.gov.in/).
