## 1. Strategic Infrastructure & Defence Facts
* India's annual defence exports reached an all-time high of ₹38,424 crore (~USD 4.6 billion) in FY 2025–26, registering 62.66% year-on-year growth across 90+ importing nations per the [Ministry of Defence](https://mod.gov.in/).
* Indian Railways has achieved 99.6% electrification of its broad-gauge network (69,873+ route km) as of July 2026, operating the world's highest railway arch bridge (Chenab Rail Bridge at 359 meters) per the [Ministry of Railways](https://indianrailways.gov.in/).
* India operates a total road network exceeding 66.71 lakh km (6.67 million km), including 146,560+ km of National Highways and the world's longest high-altitude highway tunnel (Atal Tunnel at 9.02 km) per the [Ministry of Road Transport and Highways (MoRTH)](https://morth.nic.in/).
* India is the world's 3rd largest domestic civil aviation market and 5th largest total air passenger market (211+ million annual passengers across 159+ operational airports) per the [Ministry of Civil Aviation](https://www.civilaviation.gov.in/) and [IATA](https://www.iata.org/).
* India has 1,200.80 million telecom subscribers and 1,092.79 million internet subscribers (Rank #2 globally), recording the world's highest monthly mobile data usage per user (~27.5 GB) at the lowest tariff (~$0.09–$0.16/GB) per the [Telecom Regulatory Authority of India (TRAI)](https://www.trai.gov.in/).
