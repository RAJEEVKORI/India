## 1. Core Scientific & Historical Contribution Facts
* India and individuals of Indian origin/birth have been awarded 12 Nobel Prizes across Literature, Physics, Medicine, Peace, Chemistry, and Economics per [The Nobel Foundation Official Registry](https://www.nobelprize.org/).
* Ancient Indian mathematicians formalized the decimal place-value system and explicit arithmetic rules for zero ($0$) and negative numbers in Brahmagupta's *Brahmasphutasiddhanta* (628 CE), documented by the [Indian National Science Academy (INSA)](https://insaindia.res.in/).
* Sushruta authored the *Sushruta Samhita* (~600 BCE), documenting over 300 surgical procedures, 120 surgical instruments, and pioneering reconstructive rhinoplasty and cataract surgery per the [National Institute of Indian Medical Heritage (CCRAS)](https://niimh.nic.in/).
* Indian delegate Hansa Mehta successfully amended Article 1 of the UN Universal Declaration of Human Rights in 1948 from "All men are born free and equal" to "All human beings are born free and equal," recorded by the [United Nations Archives](https://www.un.org/en/about-us/universal-declaration-of-human-rights).
