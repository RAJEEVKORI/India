## 1. Overseas Indian Diaspora & Global Welfare Facts
* India has the largest overseas diaspora population in the world at ~35.42 million (comprising ~15.85 million Non-Resident Indians [NRIs] and ~19.57 million Persons of Indian Origin [PIOs] / Overseas Citizens of India [OCIs]) per the [Ministry of External Affairs (MEA)](https://www.mea.gov.in/population-of-overseas-indians.htm).
* India is the world's #1 recipient of inward remittances, receiving USD 129.1 billion annually (with 27.7% originating from the United States and 19.2% from the UAE) per the [World Bank Migration and Development Brief](https://www.worldbank.org/) and the [Reserve Bank of India (RBI)](https://www.rbi.org.in/).
* Physicians of Indian origin account for ~9% of all practicing doctors in the United States (~100,000+ physicians) and over 11% of physicians in the UK National Health Service (NHS) per [NHS England Workforce Statistics](https://digital.nhs.uk/).
* India is the #1 cumulative troop contributor in United Nations Peacekeeping history, having deployed over 290,000 personnel across more than 50 UN missions since 1948 per [United Nations Peacekeeping](https://peacekeeping.un.org/en/india).
