## 1. Core Economic & Digital Public Infrastructure (DPI) Facts
* India's real GDP growth rate for FY 2025–26 is estimated at 7.4% (with services growing at 9.1% and manufacturing at 7.0%), making it the fastest-growing major economy globally per the [Ministry of Finance Economic Survey](https://www.indiabudget.gov.in/).
* India's foreign exchange reserves reached USD 701.4 billion in January 2026 (Rank #4 globally), providing ~11 months of import cover per the [Reserve Bank of India (RBI)](https://www.rbi.org.in/).
* India's Unified Payments Interface (UPI) processes 49% of all global real-time digital payment transactions by volume, recording 24,162 crore (241.62 billion) transactions valued at ₹314 lakh crore in FY 2025–26 per the [National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI)](https://www.npci.org.in/).
* Over 55.02 crore (550.2 million) bank accounts have been opened under the Pradhan Mantri Jan Dhan Yojana (PMJDY), with 66.5% held in rural and semi-urban regions per the [PMJDY Official Portal](https://pmjdy.gov.in/).
* Over 1.38 billion biometric digital identities have been issued under Aadhaar for direct benefit transfers and e-KYC authentication per the [Unique Identification Authority of India (UIDAI)](https://uidai.gov.in/).
